# Slice evidence: 3.8 ui-core: event routing, hit testing, focus, pointer capture

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium   **Review:** no independent review yet (recommended at phase close)

## Built
- Router: topmost visible, enabled hit test with ancestor clips and z-order; capture, target and bubble phases for pointer events; hover enter and leave (including when the hovered widget is destroyed or hidden); pointer capture with automatic release on destroy and hover frozen on the capturer; click and double-click synthesis; drag start (strictly more than 5 px) suppressing the click; focus management with Tab and Shift+Tab traversal by tab index, focus cleared on destroy or hide; key and text routing to the focused widget, then ancestors, then a global fallback; handlers are virtual objects (no std::function); nested dispatch depth limit 8.

## Verification
- ui-core.events: 146 checks including destruction inside handlers, capture on a destroyed widget, nested capture, Tab traversal with everything disabled, overlapping siblings, clipped children not hittable outside the clip; 300,000 pointer moves with hover churn in 253 ms (0.84 microseconds per event).

## Not verified and gaps
- Spatial arrow-key focus navigation and the Escape policy belong to higher layers and are not implemented here.
