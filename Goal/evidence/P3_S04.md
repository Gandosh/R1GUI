# Slice evidence: 3.4 ui-text: font loading, shaping, glyph atlas

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS (visual parity of synthetic bold not compared)   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium   **Review:** no independent review yet (recommended at phase close)

## Built
- FreeType 2.14.3 (TrueType, SFNT and the smooth rasterizer only, built from the vendored source with a private option header) and HarfBuzz 14.6.0 (single amalgamated unit, no external dependencies); both built at warning level 0, first-party code at /W4 /WX.
- Font loading with size limits and error results; shaping with kerning and ligatures (text over 1 MiB rejected, invalid UTF-8 replaced and the repaired text exposed, script runs, no bidi reordering or fallback); unhinted grayscale rasterization with quarter-pixel horizontal subpixel bins; synthetic embolden (strength size/24 down to size/32) for weights 500 and up with unchanged advances (matches the reference, owner decision); optional real Medium, SemiBold and Bold faces. CPU glyph atlas: shelf packer, default 2048x2048 R8, 1 px padding, least-recently-used eviction with pinning of current-frame glyphs, dirty-rectangle tracking, quad builder.

## Verification
- text_widths: all 576 reference widths (24 strings, sizes 10 to 16, weights 400 to 700) differ from the browser by 0.000000 px at maximum and on average; asserted at 0.01 px. Ascent, descent, line gap, x-height and cap height match assets/fonts/metrics.json for all five faces at five sizes.
- Rasterization of "Rectangle" at 12 px has the expected bounds and coverage and is bit-identical across runs; atlas no-overlap, eviction and full cases; hostile inputs (empty, 10 MB string, invalid UTF-8, lone surrogates, NUL, 100k combining marks, 100k-glyph runs, zero, negative and NaN sizes, missing and truncated font files). Glyph shapes inspected with the ui-text-dump helper.

## Not verified and gaps
- The atlas had never been uploaded to a GPU when this slice closed (done in slice 3.12); the synthetic-bold strength was not compared with the browser visually; clang-cl and sanitizers were not run.
- Not covered: Prepend characters, Indic conjuncts, complete Extended_Pictographic data, CJK and Thai word breaking; right-to-left caret behavior only with synthetic runs.
