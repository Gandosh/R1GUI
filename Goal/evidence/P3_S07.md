# Slice evidence: 3.7 ui-core: flexbox-style layout (own implementation)

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium   **Review:** no independent review yet (recommended at phase close)

## Built
- Own flexbox subset (owner decision: no third-party engine): row and column and their reverses, wrap, justify-content, align-items and align-self (baseline lays out as start), gap, grow, shrink and basis with the freezing loop, px, percent, auto and fit-content sizes with measure callbacks, min and max sizes, padding, margins with auto main-axis margins, absolute positioning with insets, display none, overflow clip flag. Rounding: absolute edges rounded half-up and sizes are edge differences (no 1 px gaps); values clamped to plus or minus 1e9; NaN, infinite and negative inputs sanitized. Unsupported by design: grid, float, margin collapsing, writing modes, right-to-left, automatic minimum content size, percent padding and margins.

## Verification
- ui-core.layout: 463 checks with hand-computed CSS results; a full pass over 10,001 nodes (10,001 commits, 6,600 measure callbacks) takes 14.4 to 19.7 ms best of 7; 40 nested content-sized containers take 5 measure calls.
- The property-panel example in docs/spec/widgets.md does not add up (114 + 6 + 114 + 6 + 26 = 266, not 234); the test covers three consistent readings with exact pixels (fields 114, 6, 114 in 234; field plus rail 202, 6, 26; the two-rail case shrinking fields to 98).

## Not verified and gaps
- The freeze loop can be quadratic when many items freeze one per iteration (not measured). Layout cost at 100k nodes and wrap-heavy cases were not profiled.
