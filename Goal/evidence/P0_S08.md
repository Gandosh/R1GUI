## Finding
`reference/OpenPencil/LICENSE` is the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Danila Poyarkov. MIT permits use, copy, modify and distribution of the software, including design tokens and CSS values, provided the copyright and permission notice are retained in copies or substantial portions.

## Actions required
- Keep the OpenPencil MIT notice in `third_party/NOTICES.md` (or `docs/third-party.md`) when tokens or derived assets ship.
- Fonts, icons (Lucide, ISC) and any other assets inside OpenPencil have their own licenses; check each one in slices 1.4 and 1.5 before use.
- Code stays original: only tokens and visual values are reused, not source structure.
