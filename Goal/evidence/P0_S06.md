# Slice evidence: 0.6 Decide text strategy

**Status:** OWNER ACCEPTED   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium

## Decision
FreeType (rasterization) + HarfBuzz (shaping) with our own glyph atlas on Vulkan. Single-line Latin editing first; OS text services / IME and complex-script editing later (plan risk register).

## Consequences
- Slice 3.4 builds font loading, shaping and the atlas; 3.5 single-line editing.
- FreeType is used under the FTL option (not GPL). Both libraries enter the allowlist (0.7).
- Matching OpenPencil's browser text exactly uses the same font files plus a tolerance (slices 1.5, 1.6).

## Unresolved issues
- IME and complex-script editing deferred; accessibility absent in v1.
