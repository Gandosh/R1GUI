# Slice evidence: 3.3 ui-render: 2D batcher (rects, rounded rects, borders, shadows, clipping)

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium   **Review:** no independent review yet (recommended at phase close)

## Built
- Painter (no OS or Vulkan types in its header): fill rectangles, per-corner rounded rectangles, inside borders, CSS-style box shadows (offset, blur, spread, colour; gaussian rounded-box integration with knock-out), clip stack with nested intersection, per-draw opacity, textured quads (R8 tinted glyphs, RGBA images), a line helper. Two pipelines (SDF shapes, textured), SPIR-V compiled with glslc at build time and embedded, dynamic scissor, per-frame ring buffer, consecutive draws batched. Limit of 65536 instances per frame with a clear error; NaN and infinite arguments are skipped and counted.
- The painter's CPU part builds as its own library without the Vulkan SDK.

## Verification
- Fast tier: painter math (clip stack, SDF reference, instance packing). GPU tier (15 cases): exact fills, rounded-rectangle anti-aliasing, border width, shadow symmetry and bounds, clip correctness, alpha blending, textured quad tint, batching reduces draw calls, hostile inputs, texture lifetime.
- A panel with radius 8, border #3a3a3a and shadow lg compared with an analytic reference: max error 1.01 of 255, mean 0.045 (tolerances 3 and 0.35). Renderings of the shadow and panel tests were viewed by the builder.
- 2000 rounded rectangles at 1280x720: 1 draw call, painter CPU 0.20 ms, record plus submit plus wait 0.49 ms (Release).

## Not verified and gaps
- Pixel comparison against the real OpenPencil reference crops starts in slice 3.12 and Phase 4.
- Text drawing through the textured pipeline was exercised only with synthetic textures until slice 3.12.
