# Slice evidence: 1.5 Fonts and text metrics

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Low

- Fonts: Inter 4.001 (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold) copied to `assets/fonts/` with `OFL.txt` (SIL OFL 1.1, copyright The Inter Project Authors; licence body taken from the OFL text in the OpenPencil test fixtures, header from the font's own name table).
- Metrics: `python tools/spec/font_metrics.py` wrote `assets/fonts/metrics.json` (units per em 2048, hhea ascender 1984, descender -494, line gap 0, x-height 1118, cap height 1490; ASCII advance widths per face; bounds-checked sfnt parsing).
- Browser text oracle: `node tools/spec/text_reference.mjs ...` wrote `tests/reference/text-widths.json` (576 widths: 24 strings at sizes 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16 and weights 400, 500, 600, 700).
- Cross-check: "Rectangle" at 12 px measures 56.47 px in the browser and 56.59 px as the unkerned font-unit sum for Regular (0.12 px difference, consistent with kerning).

## Finding (affects Phases 3 and 4)
The DOM UI loads only the Inter Regular face. Headings and tabs at weights 500 to 700 are browser-synthesized bold with the same advance widths as Regular (measured 56.48 px for "Rectangle" at 12 px for 400, 600 and 700). The toolkit either reproduces the synthesized look or uses the real Medium and SemiBold faces with slightly different widths; decide in slice 3.4. Whether the CanvasKit design surface uses the real heavier faces was not verified.
