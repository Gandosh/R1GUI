# Slice evidence: 3.5 ui-text: single-line editing

**Status:** TECHNICAL PASS   **Date:** 2026-10-09   **Risk tier:** Medium   **Review:** no independent review yet (recommended at phase close)

## Built
- TextEditor model: UTF-8 buffer with maximum length (1 MiB default, 16 MiB cap; over-length insertions cut at a grapheme boundary), caret and selection always on grapheme boundaries (multi-byte, Mn/Me/Mc, variation selectors, ZWJ emoji, regional-indicator pairs, Hangul), insert, delete by grapheme and word, movement and selection extension, select all, double-click word and triple-click all, copy, cut and paste through callbacks, grouped undo and redo, IME preedit state, read-only mode, caret position from the shaped run, x-to-caret hit test, horizontal scroll to keep the caret visible. Control characters, NUL, CR, LF, TAB and U+2028/2029 are dropped from the single-line buffer.

## Verification
- Editor tests plus a fuzz run: 12 runs of 10,000 operations (4 seeds, 3 length limits) hold invariants (caret in bounds, valid UTF-8, on a boundary, length limit) with an undo and redo round trip around each edit; ZWJ and flag sequences, combining marks, invalid UTF-8 sanitizing, 1 MB paste.

## Not verified and gaps
- No GPU or OS clipboard involvement in this slice; interactive typing is shown in the slice 3.12 preview. Word boundaries for CJK and Thai are not implemented.
