## Deviations and gaps
- Real hang risk not caused here but seen here: one of five desktop runs hung the preview (not responding, 0 % CPU) after the creator window closed. A separate builder is fixing a hang in native window creation and destruction (`WindowTarget.cpp`: `vkDeviceWaitIdle` on the first swapchain of a window; `NativeFloatingBackend::destroyWindow` hiding the window first). **The desktop drives, tests and captures above ran with those two uncommitted changes applied in the build tree; the committed branch does not contain them.** Without them the owner can meet that hang when closing a floating window.
- The old `editor_test` customize test and the Quick Actions panel, Edit > Customize and the Customize tool strip are gone; panel id 8 is unused (stored layouts drop it).
- Hotkey editor in a window narrower than 860 px stacks its columns and its caption lines overlap (seen at 562 px); the preview opens it at 1100 px.
- Not verified: windows on a monitor with another display scale, snap layouts (as before).
